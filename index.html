2026 – Present

External research collaboration with a Texas A&M University research group

  • Re-ran 830 BrowseComp-Plus benchmark questions, reproducing 41.81% single-agent and 44.70% dual-agent accuracy under a semantic-judge metric. These results served as reference baselines for further experiments.
